The idea in one page
An embodied agent enacts and submits, or responds and requests. An authoritative body approves or
denies. An authoritative agent records and reviews, returning an account. These positions describe
functions, including when one institution performs several of them.
The nine triads form an ordered social loop. The cube is an additional representation of bodies 2-9: a
three-bit code records a proposed relationship to the three positions. Loop 1 preserves all eight
assignments checked in the supplied discussion. Other loops are alternative assignments, not
automatic replacements for that reference.
CURRENT TERMINOLOGY
Body 5 now uses Preservational for its reviewing position; body 9 uses Cultural for its acting
position. Environmental is reserved as the collective name at code 010. The role names and
body order stay consistent across both documents.
A separate Elaboration and Logic Companion records the source choices, mathematical checks, semantic limits, and
reasons for the editorial decisions. All worked cases remain hypothetical.

“Embodied agent” identifies the acting position; it need not imply that every operation is performed by
one solitary person.
The authoritative agent’s account also need not wait until an action has finished. Existing records and
current conditions can inform a request, an approval, an ongoing intervention, or a later review.
An agent acts or requests; a body authorises or denies; an authoritative
agent records and reviews. The returned account informs what happens
next.
That is the recurring relationship. It does not require every interaction to follow an inflexible three-step
timetable.
Three different questions. An action shows that something happened. An approval establishes that a
designated body permitted it. A reviewed account supplies information about the conditions, process,
and consequences. None automatically substitutes for the other two.

PART THREE
Refinements that shaped the model
Development logistics
Includes architecture, design, planning proposals, construction, maintenance, and movement. Transport is one
expression of it; its broader function is to organise and carry out changes in the material world.
Property stewardship
Supplies the corresponding account of rights and responsibilities: land, buildings, tenure, district and location
records, personal possessions, ownership claims, use rights, and asset condition. Maintaining a building and
maintaining the account of who owns it are distinguishable functions, even when one organisation handles both.
Sanitation concerns whether energy and resource activity is appropriately contained and responsibly handled.
environmental outcome remain different things: permission does not establish that the resulting conditions were
acceptable.
Nutritional provision
Extends beyond farming to production, preparation, availability, consumption, diets, and nutritional requirements. Its
position concerns the recorded and reviewed conditions of nourishment. Producing a meal remains an action; the
authoritative function is accounting for provision and its relationship to requirements. Pharmaceuticals were not
settled as an umbrella label and are not reinstated here.
Financial
Covers accounts, capital, transactions, obligations, investment positions, and related records. The bank example
resolves the overlap: financial accounting and commercial approval can coexist within one institution while
remaining separate functions. Deciding whether to extend a loan is commercial; maintaining and reviewing the
account is financial.
Content creation, media distribution, news journalism
Separates producing expression, deciding its distribution, and investigating or reporting an account. The distinction
follows the purpose and function of the activity, not whether someone publishes words or video: an influencer can do
reporting; a journalist can perform for entertainment.
Diplomatic
Not an examiner of the home government alone. The delegated representative reviews the nation’s position in
relation to other nations’ laws and affairs and to the external boundaries and capacities within which it operates.
Constitutional
Closer to constitutional adjudication, in the manner of a supreme court reviewing governmental action. Approval
means permission, not endorsement of the policy.

PART FIVE
Worked example: the
water-contamination case
Residents of a shared water supply have reason to suspect contamination. The question is not whether
the model can describe the problem, but how the problem travels: who acts, who permits, and who
keeps the account at each stage.
How the case begins
 #
Position
What happens
1
Content creation
A resident uploads a water-concern video. That is embodied action and
submission.
2
Media distribution
Permits circulation. Participating users approve or reject the proposition through
their responses; rejection can also make it spread.
3
News journalism
Records and reviews both the claim and the response, checking evidence and
returning a more developed account to the public.
4
Families, workplaces, and neighbours organise a community meeting and
request precautions.
5
Educational
At the meeting, a speaker or public announcement endorses or rejects proposed
guidance and explains why.
6
Religious
Asks how the response fits inherited understandings of Creator, reality, truth,
and obligations to others: was our response truthful and responsible; does
protecting others require me to reconsider my conduct or my interpretation?
Faith frames the reflection but does not prove the facts. Test results constrain claims about the water; ethical
commitments inform responsibility; the review brings the two together into an account.
The return into government
The review might produce a shared conviction: “Protecting our neighbours requires honest disclosure of
dangers to the water we share.” People may agree on that responsibility while disagreeing about its
interpretation or implementation. Through participation, advocacy, voting, and representation, those
convictions inform what people ask government to do.
Religious communities contribute alongside other communities and nonreligious citizens; a religious
community expresses part of the public’s convictions and does not automatically constitute the majority
or speak for everyone. The connection works without that equivalence.
What is moving through the sequence. It is our interpretation of reality, not the physical regularities
themselves. “Law” is used broadly: what becomes canonical or coherent within an inherited tradition
(teachings, principles, accepted interpretations), together with the foundational account of reality: principles,
established findings, results of screenings and observations. A ruling or a doctrine settles a question within its
authority; it does not determine physical reality.
Resolving into new interpretations or new laws. A new interpretation can emerge within the religious review
(what our duty requires now) and feed back into conduct and guidance without becoming a state statute. If
protection still looks inadequate, the account can go forward as a request for a new law: governmental agents draft
and submit, constitutional authorises. Any resulting law stays open to review, and the cycle continues.

The pattern. A blocked journalistic account can enter community circulation and potentially return as public
pressure, a legal claim, or a demand for governmental response. A refusal in one domain need not end the
matter if another legitimate route remains available.
Two qualifications
Accessibility. The community route must actually be usable. If the same gatekeeper controls it too, its alternative
name does not create an alternative path.
Circulation is not truth. A truthful account and a false rumour can both become trends. The route preserves the
possibility of being heard; investigation, evidence, and review remain necessary for assessing the claim.

PART NINE
Five connected propositions
1
Acting, permitting, and accounting answer different questions.
An action demonstrates that something happened. An approval establishes that a designated body
permitted it. A reviewed account supplies information about conditions, process, and consequences.
None substitutes for the other two.
2
Functional distinction makes responsibility easier to locate.
When an operation fails, the model helps separate an inadequate proposal, an unjustified approval, poor
execution, and an incomplete or misleading account. Several can occur together, but they are different
failures requiring different responses.
3
The account must remain capable of informing subsequent activity.
Records that cannot reach anyone able to respond have limited corrective value. Review informs further
requests, decisions, corrections, or challenges.
4
Every domain depends on conditions it cannot produce alone.
Public authority requires implementation; implementation requires infrastructure, resources, and people;
sustaining people requires health, nourishment, goods, and economic coordination; accountability
requires communication and social engagement.
5
Connections between domains can provide recourse when a local process
fails.
The journalism-to-community route demonstrates the principle. Additional routes elsewhere in the
framework are still to be investigated and have not all been agreed.
Together these explain why the framework is more than a list of professions: it combines a classification
of capabilities with a proposed account of how social activity is sustained and made answerable.

What needs to be specified in use
Influence must be relative to someone, over a stated decision, at a stated time, and under a stated
