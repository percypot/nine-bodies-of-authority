Prepared 27 October 2026, revised the same day against the two rubrics. Version 6 is a plan. No Version 6 HTML exists yet. `v6-claude-prompt.md` is the spec; where this file and the prompt differ, the prompt wins. This file records no score for Version 5 or Version 6.

## The sum

State it once, as the lede, in the corrected name key and in the Comprehensive name frame:

8 codes + 27 ledger names + 27 bodies = 62

The three addends follow in that order. The domain eights are not a twenty-seven inside it. The three role names are not a fourth addend. The count is not 81. 24-plus-3 and the set of sets stay proposed, undrawn and outside the sixty-two. 24-plus-3 is the count of the sequence plus meta; the set of sets is its label.

## Codes · 8

The eight codes sit on bodies 2 to 9. Body 1 carries no code.

101 Consequential on body 2. 111 Deterministic on body 3. 011 Propositional on body 4. 010 Environmental on body 5. 110 Reproducible on body 6. 100 Contractual on body 7. 000 Transmissible on body 8. 001 Transformational on body 9.

Environmental is the code on body 5. Preservational is the body name in body 5's authoritative-agent column. They stay in those two places.

No code is a meta label. That question is closed.

## Ledger names · 27

- Positions: Supposition, Exposition, Reposition, Juxtaposition, Proposition, Composition, Decomposition, Deposition.

## Bodies · 27

The twenty-seven are the body names in Version 4's table, from Governmental, Constitutional, Diplomatic to Cultural, Educational, Religious. The three role lenses sit on top, in Version 4's column order:

- Embodied agent: enacts and submits.

The roles are the meta layer of the bodies. They are not names 28, 29 and 30. The count is not eighty-one.

Analogy stays on the ledger categorical eight. Liken stays on the categorical domain eight. The two names are not merged. No code is a meta label.
