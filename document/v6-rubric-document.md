# Rubric for the five-tab document

Instrument for *The Nine Bodies of Authority*. Version 4 is the grammar baseline. Score a five-tab document on the six dimensions below. This file records no score for Version 5 and no score for Version 6.

Give each dimension one score from 0 to 4. Report the six scores side by side. Leave them uncombined. A strong score on one dimension leaves the others as they are.

The document under review is one file with five modes, in this order, Interactive first: Interactive, Illustrative, Comprehensive, Descriptive, Transcriptive.

## 1. Conceptual originality

New relations among enactment, decision, and the returned account, made with the locked names still in their places.

| Score | What the document does |
|---|---|
| 4 | The triad, the census, and the ledger each make a relation a reader could not get from a list of institutions. |
| 3 | Those relations are present. One of them is stated once and left unused by the figures or the case. |
| 2 | The nine bodies are a renamed civics list, or the ledger repeats the bodies in different words. |
| 1 | A single sentence claims a system. The tabs illustrate a familiar domain list. |
| 0 | The document borrows an outside theory and leaves the nine bodies as examples of that theory. |

### Top score

A 4 shows three relations at once.

- A body of authority is the whole triad. The embodied agent enacts and submits. The authoritative body approves or denies. The authoritative agent records, reviews, and returns the account. The approver is one position inside the body. The returned account decides what the embodied agent does next.
- The census sorts names by kind. Eight codes, twenty-seven ledger names, and twenty-seven body names are three kinds. The domain eights sit beside that census as a fourth arrangement of eights, with their own meta labels.
- The ledger is an instrument of positions, categorical names, and contingent names. The three role lenses are how a reader looks at a body. Disposition, Imposition, and Superposition are lane names inside the ledger twenty-seven. Each of these jobs stays visible as its own job.

Originality sits in those relations. A new sentence earns the score when it makes one of them sharper.

### Failure modes

- The opening list of social domains is paraphrased and offered as the discovery.
- A synonym is treated as a concept. Liken stands in as a spelling of Analogy, or a code is given a second name and called a finding.
- The count is asked to do the work of the idea. Eighty-one, or 24-plus-3 folded into the sixty-two, is presented as the contribution.
- An interaction is novel because it moves an event onto another position. The placement itself is the claimed idea.
- One role is written as the whole body, and the other two roles are described as helpers.

## 2. Significance

Whether a reader can place a real action with the frame, and whether that placement changes what authority means.

| Score | What the document does |
|---|---|
| 4 | A reader can place an action in a body, a role, and a code, and can say what the ledger records about it. The placement changes the next decision. |
| 3 | The placement is possible. One tab shows it, and the other tabs leave it as a definition. |
| 2 | The frame describes institutions. It does not change an approval, a record, or a request. |
| 1 | Significance is asserted by size: nine bodies, sixty-two names, or 459 turns. |
| 0 | The document is a private glossary. Nothing outside the glossary follows from it. |

### Top score

A 4 makes the returned account do work.

- The reader can take an action through the water case and name the body, the role that acts, the role that decides, and the role that returns the account.
- The same action meets a code on bodies 2 through 9, and the code says what is in focus.
- The public ledger records the position, the categorical name, and the release, or the position, the contingent name, and the archive line for the role in view.
- Power inside a triad stays power over that action. One triad does not rule another triad.
- After the account returns, the embodied agent responds and requests. The document shows that the account belongs to the agent who receives it.

### Failure modes

- The water case narrates the topic and never lands on a body.
- Definitions are complete and unused. No passage, figure, or ledger inspection applies them.
- The transcript is asked to prove the system because it is long.
- The authoritative body is treated as the institution that matters, and the returned account is an afterword.
- Two bodies are ranked, and the ranking is called the finding.

## 3. Quality of prose

Declarative prose in Interactive, Illustrative, Comprehensive, and Descriptive. Transcriptive stays verbatim and is outside this standard.

| Score | What the document does |
|---|---|
| 4 | Sentences state what a role does. Near-names stay distinct. New lines are short and specific. The transcript is untouched. |
| 3 | The prose is declarative. One passage blurs a pair the name key keeps apart, while the key itself stays exact. |
| 2 | Hedges carry the argument, or several near-names collapse inside Comprehensive. |
| 1 | Slogans and restatement replace sentences that name an actor and an action. |
| 0 | The transcript is rewritten into the house voice, or the other tabs are written as transcript. |

### Top score

A 4 reads as statement.

- Sentences are declarative. *May* appears only as permission. The words *might*, *arguably*, *perhaps*, and *seems* are absent from the four authored tabs.
- *Enact* is the verb for giving something formal effect. The embodied agent enacts and submits. The prose keeps that verb when it means formal effect.
- Near-names stay in their sentences. Proposition is a ledger position. Propositional is the code on body 4. Environmental is the code on body 5. Preservational is the authoritative-agent name on body 5. Analogy is the ledger categorical name at Juxtaposition. Liken is the fourth name of the categorical domain eight.
- The sum is said as `8 codes + 27 ledger names + 27 bodies = 62`, addends in that order, when the census is introduced.
- Node labels stay at four words at most.
- Comprehensive carries Version 4’s prose. A sentence added beside it is specific. Unfinished work sits on a list headed *Next*.
- Transcriptive is exempt. Fillers, misspellings, hedges, and the word *Claude* inside a turn stay as spoken or typed.

### Failure modes

- A hedge softens a definition the frame has already fixed.
- A sentence calls Liken a form of Analogy, or calls Propositional the position.
- The sum is introduced as “the sixty-two” with the addends missing or reordered.
- Descriptive adds a moral to the water case, or Comprehensive explains the transcript.
- An editor smooths a turn, cuts a filler, or corrects a misspelling in Transcriptive.
- A mode name grows an *-al* form. The tab names stay Interactive, Illustrative, Comprehensive, Descriptive, and Transcriptive.

## 4. Idea coherence

One system, visible in every tab, with each name doing one job everywhere it appears.

| Score | What the document does |
|---|---|
| 4 | The triad, the eight codes, the ledger twenty-seven, the body twenty-seven, and the domain eights agree across all five tabs. |
| 3 | They agree. One cross-reference points at the right body and the wrong role, and the name key still has the role right. |
| 2 | Two tabs describe two systems. A reader cannot tell which names belong to the census. |
| 1 | Each tab is locally consistent and silent about the others. |
| 0 | Names migrate. A code, a lane, a role, and a body name trade places between tabs. |

### Top score

A 4 holds one circuit.

- Every tab uses the same triad. Embodied agent, authoritative body, authoritative agent stay in that column order wherever the bodies are printed.
- The eight codes on bodies 2 through 9 match the ledger’s positions, categorical names, and contingent names without becoming those names.
- Domain eights use the same position list and the same contingent list as the ledger, and they use Liken where the ledger uses Analogy. The document states that the two names occupy two places.
- The water case enacts the nine bodies in the same order Comprehensive names them. A descriptive passage ties to its comprehensive passage.
- An Interactive inspection shows the categorical name or the contingent name the name key prints for that position and that role.
- Way one, way two, and way three stay the three routes Version 4 draws. The bodies-by-ways matrix leaves way two and way three empty.
- Body 1 carries three role names and no code.

### Failure modes

- Interactive presses a role the figure labels as a lane, and Comprehensive calls that lane a role.
- The categorical domain eight and the ledger categorical eight print one word in both places.
- Descriptive tells the water case for a body whose comprehensive passage describes a different body.
- The three role names reappear as extra bodies, or as three of the eight codes.
- A matrix fills way two or way three, and the prose still says those ways are open.
- The public ledger’s centre events and the name key’s categorical eight diverge, and neither place is marked as the source.

## 5. Structural integrity of the five tabs

The five modes are one document. Each mode does its own work, and the shared reference still opens the mode it names. The dimension score follows the weakest tab.

| Score | What the document does |
|---|---|
| 4 | All five tabs meet the descriptions below. Interactive opens first. A stored reference opens its mode. |
| 3 | All five tabs are present and doing their own work. One control, figure, or cross-link is missing, and the tab is still usable. |
| 2 | A tab is present and collapsed into another tab, or one required region of Interactive or Transcriptive is empty. |
| 1 | One or two tabs carry the document. The others are labels. |
| 0 | A tab is gone, a sixth mode is added, or Interactive is no longer the opening mode. |

### Top score

A 4 keeps each tab in its job.

**Interactive.** The travelling map is live. One window, *One path of eight*, sits on the eight codes: scale 8 is the whole path and starts pressed, scale 3 is the current station and its two neighbours, scale 1 is the current station. The public ledger is on this tab. The matrixes are on this tab: Version 4’s matrixes, the domain matrixes, and bodies-by-ways. Body 1 is blank on the codes and on bodies-by-ways. The ledger controls are three role lenses, with authoritative body pressed at the start. A click on a centre event inspects the position, the categorical name, and the release, and leaves the pressed role as it is. A click on a dashed branch previews the position, the contingent name, and that role’s archive line, and a second click closes the preview. Branch clicks leave the pressed role as it is. There is one such window. There is no placement control.

**Illustrative.** Version 4’s figures are here, including the cube. One further figure shows the ledger of eight plus sixteen, the eight codes, and the nine triads. The figure’s three lines are Disposition, Imposition, and Superposition. Those line names are the figure. The role lenses remain the controls on Interactive. 24-plus-3 and the set of sets are marked proposed, and they are undrawn.

**Comprehensive.** Version 4’s prose is here. The original name key remains under the heading *The collective names*. The corrected 62 name key sits beside that key. The corrected key is the census. It does not delete *The collective names*. The sum is the lede of the name frame. The domain eights follow in their own block.

**Descriptive.** The water case is nine passages, one for each body, in body order. Each passage enacts that body and ties to its Comprehensive passage. The nine passages remain when any further passage is added. A further passage is named before it appears.

**Transcriptive.** The tab holds the 459-turn record of Untitled document (15). Kickers are `VOICE` or `TEXT`. `CLAUDE · TEXT` appears only on the turns that already print `CLAUDE · TEXT`. The session line reads `Session 1 · 459 turns · time not recorded`. The turns are the record. The 127-turn Percy log is a different file and stays out of this tab.

### Failure modes

- Interactive opens on another tab, or the hash ignores a stored mode.
- A second one-three-eight window appears, or the window is built on something other than the eight codes.
- The role pills are labelled Disposition, Imposition, and Superposition.
- A placement control, a drag, or a second pill moves an event onto another position.
- Centre clicks or branch clicks change which role is pressed.
- Illustrative draws 24-plus-3, or drops the cube, or uses the ledger figure as a second set of controls.
- Comprehensive replaces *The collective names* with the corrected key.
- Descriptive becomes a second essay, or the nine water passages shrink.
- Transcriptive is a summary, a selection, or a rebuild from the 127-turn log.
- Speaker names are added to kickers that print only `VOICE` or `TEXT`.
- The tabs are reordered, or a sixth mode is added.

## 6. Fidelity to the locked frame

The census, the lenses, the domain eights, the ledger names, the transcript record, and the public ledger. A 4 keeps every item in this section. One broken item holds the score at 2 or below.

| Score | What the document does |
|---|---|
| 4 | Every locked item below is present, in the place named, with the names named. |
| 3 | Every locked item holds. One of them is easy to miss because a heading or a caption carries it alone, and the names in that place are still the locked names. |
| 2 | One locked item is broken: a name has moved, a count has changed, the record is the wrong record, or the public ledger is gone. |
| 1 | Several locked items are broken. The document still shows a nine-body outline. |
| 0 | The sum, the ledger, or the 459-turn record has been replaced. |

### Top score

A 4 keeps the frame exactly.

**The sum.** The census is `8 codes + 27 ledger names + 27 bodies = 62`. The three addends appear in that order. The domain eights are beside the sum, in their own block, and they are not a twenty-seven inside it. The three role names are not a fourth addend. The count is 62. 24-plus-3 and the set of sets stay outside the sixty-two.

**The eight codes.** They sit on bodies 2 through 9. Body 1 is blank. No code is written on body 1.

**The ledger twenty-seven.** Eight positions, eight categorical names, eight contingent names, and three lanes.

### Failure modes

- The sum is 81, or the roles are added as a fourth addend, or the domain eights are counted as one of the twenty-sevens.
- 24-plus-3 or the set of sets is added into the sixty-two.
- A code is printed on body 1, or a code is called a meta label, or blank slots remain for meta-codes.
- The categorical domain eight prints Analogy. The ledger categorical eight prints Liken.
- Disposition, Imposition, or Superposition is a ledger control, or any of those three names is dropped from the ledger twenty-seven in the name key.
- The public ledger is removed or renamed.
- A body name is swapped across the role columns, or Preservational is moved onto the code and Environmental onto the body.
- Proposition and Propositional are treated as one name.
- Transcriptive uses a different record, prints clock times, or adds speaker names beyond the `CLAUDE · TEXT` kickers already on the page.
- A retired-term sweep removes the public ledger or renames the five modes.
