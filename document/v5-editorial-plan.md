# Version 5 editorial plan

## Sum

Write the sum, in the name key and in the Comprehensive name frame, as this line:

8 codes + 27 ledger names + 27 bodies = 62

Say it once as the lede. The three addends follow in that order. Do not call the domain eights a twenty-seven. Do not put the three role names inside the count.

## Ledger twenty-seven

Leave the ledger list as it stands: eight positions, eight categorical names, eight contingent names, and the three lanes Disposition, Imposition, and Superposition. Analogy remains the fourth categorical name. Proposition remains the fifth position. Propositional remains the third code. Keep the public ledger as the name of that ledger. Do not retire the term, and do not swap it for another.

## Bodies twenty-seven

The twenty-seven are the body names only. The order runs Governmental, Constitutional, Diplomatic through Cultural, Educational, Religious, in the nine groups Version 4 already uses.

On top of each body, show the three role lenses: embodied agent, authoritative body, authoritative agent. Those roles are the meta layer. They belong to the bodies. They are not twenty-eight, twenty-nine, and thirty, and they do not turn the twenty-seven into eighty-one.

The name under each lens follows Version 4’s table and no other pairing. Version 4 already heads its columns embodied agent, authoritative body, and authoritative agent, and it already places Governmental, Constitutional, and Diplomatic in that order, and the same way through Cultural, Educational, and Religious. Repeat that order. Do not invent another.

## Domain eights, beside the sum

Give the domain eights their own block after the sum, not inside it. Head them logical, categorical, and contingent. Those three words are their meta labels. The categorical column uses Liken, not Analogy. State that no code is a meta label, and that the question is closed. Delete the blank three-slot table and every sentence that says three of the eight codes are the meta labels.

Keep Analogy and Liken as two names in two places. Do not call one a spelling of the other.

## Outside the sum

24-plus-3 and the set of sets stay proposed, and they stay undrawn. They are not added into the sixty-two.

## Voice

Declarative sentences. No might, arguably, perhaps, or seems. The locked Version 4 grammar stays: five modes, Interactive first, the same tokens and components. This pass does not restyle the document and does not sweep other terms.
